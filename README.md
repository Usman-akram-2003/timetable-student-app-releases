# Student Timetable App

## [⬇ Download the latest app (APK)](https://github.com/Usman-akram-2003/timetable-student-app-releases/releases/latest/download/TimetableStudent.apk)

1. Tap the link above on your Android phone.
2. Open the downloaded file and tap **Install**. If your phone asks, allow installs from your browser.
3. Open the app and sign in with the **Institute ID** and **password** from your institute.

You only do this once. After that the app updates itself: when a new version is out it shows **Update required**. Tap **Update now**, then **Install**.

All versions: [Releases](https://github.com/Usman-akram-2003/timetable-student-app-releases/releases)
